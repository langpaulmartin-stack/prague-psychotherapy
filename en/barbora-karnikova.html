Barbora Kárníková – psychotherapist

I believe each of us has the ability to live a fulfilling life, while our starting points and experiences shape how we feel and where we find ourselves. Therapy helps to untangle questions we often cannot answer alone, slow down, and create space for change. Choosing therapy is a brave step, and I respect every such decision. In my work I value authenticity, curiosity, understanding, and respect for the diversity of life paths.

About Me

I was drawn to social work and later psychotherapy through my temperament and family background. Both fields relate to relationships, which are essential to me. How we relate to ourselves, our family, partners, work and the world strongly influences our quality of life. That is why I emphasize the therapeutic relationship and its healing potential. In my life I find joy in my children, family and friends, time in nature, and ongoing personal development.

Education & Therapeutic Approach

I completed the experiential part of the psychodynamic training SUR‑KUŠ (2014–2019). I enjoy ongoing learning, exploring different therapeutic approaches and integrating what fits my practice. I am a candidate member of the Czech Association for Psychotherapy and work under regular supervision. I also work part‑time as a coordinator of social services in the specialized Beztíže program at DDM Ulita, where I provide short‑term low‑threshold therapy for adolescents and young adults in challenging life situations.

Courses:

  • Working with traumatized clients (2026)

  • Biosynthesis for work with children and adolescents (2024)
