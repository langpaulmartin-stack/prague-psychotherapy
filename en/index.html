Additional Information

Ethical Standards

I follow the ethical code of the Czech Association for Psychotherapy. At the first session we sign a psychotherapy agreement that defines all aspects of the therapeutic relationship. I work under regular accredited supervision.

Prague Psychotherapy – Mission & Values

I help clients of different ages and backgrounds find and develop their potential, exploring both the strengths and challenges of inner experience.

Care is centered on each client’s unique needs and story. In a safe environment I listen and we work together to improve mental health and quality of life.

Privacy Policy

Therapeutic work is strictly confidential. No personal data are shared with third parties. All processing follows the ethical code and our privacy policy.
