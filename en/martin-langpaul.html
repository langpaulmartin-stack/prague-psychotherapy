Martin Langpaul – psychotherapist

I was born in Prague, but life has taken me to many countries around the world. I trained as a teacher (music and German) and have a wide range of interests — from the environment to digital automation and sport. I worked for many years in international organizations and today, alongside psychotherapy, I also provide consulting for companies.

I came to psychotherapy through experience in organizations focused on youth education. My own international experience allows me to work with clients facing challenges connected with life abroad or returning home.

Education & Therapeutic Approach

  • University of West Bohemia (2002–2007)
  • University of Leipzig (2006–2007)
  • Comprehensive psychotherapeutic training (2019–2026)

I graduated from the accredited depth psychodynamic SUR training within the community of Dr. Michael Chytrý and Dr. Věra Rašková. As part of this training I completed the experiential, theoretical and supervisory pillars between 2019 and 2026. In 2026 I completed a one‑year music therapy course led by Dr. Matěj Lipský, in collaboration with Jiří Kantor and Jana Weber.

  • I work under professional supervision of accredited supervisors focused on psychodynamic and psychoanalytic work with clients.
  • In therapy I emphasise a safe and authentic relationship, attending to the client's personal and cultural context.
  • I am particularly interested in how personal and cultural context affects experience, especially for people with international backgrounds or living abroad.
