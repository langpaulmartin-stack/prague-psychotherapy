Supervision Dates & Registration

Supervision is intended for trainees who have completed the experiential part of training and have at least 350 hours of experiential training. Registration no later than 14 days before the date; cancellation at least 7 days in advance.

Organization & payments: We add participants after payment is received. Late cancellation (< 7 days) forfeits the fee. Minimum number of participants is 4.
